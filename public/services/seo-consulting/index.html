In short

SEO consulting gives your team senior direction without outsourcing the work: an independent audit, a prioritised roadmap, review before changes go live, and help when a decision is unclear. It suits teams that already have developers or writers but need someone accountable for search decisions.

Process

A straightforward sequence

The exact scope is agreed before work begins, but the shape stays consistent.

  1. 01

    Align

    Clarify commercial goals, team structure, and the decisions that need support.

  2. 02

    Review

    Inspect data, site architecture, content, and current ways of working.

  3. 03

    Recommend

    Present options with trade-offs rather than a generic best-practice list.

  4. 04

    Support

    Stay available for implementation questions, QA, and monthly recalibration.
