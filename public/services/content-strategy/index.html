Service

Content Strategy

Publishing more is rarely the answer. A useful content strategy makes each page's job, audience, and connection to the wider site explicit.

Focused scopePractical deliverablesClear handover

Best for

Content and marketing teams that need a defensible publishing plan rather than a list of keywords.

Discuss this service →

What is covered

Practical work, clearly scoped

Search-led content strategy covering topic clusters, intent mapping, briefs, internal links, and content refreshes.

01

Content audit

Decide what to keep, improve, consolidate, or remove.

02

Topic clusters

Group related demand around clear pillar and supporting pages.

03

Intent mapping

Match pages to research, comparison, decision, and support intent.

04

Editorial briefs

Give writers search evidence, structure, questions, and internal-link targets.

05

Refresh cycle

Set a practical cadence for updating pages that already earn visibility.

Deliverables

What you keep

  • ✓Content inventory with actions
  • ✓Topic cluster and keyword-to-URL map
  • ✓Reusable brief template
  • ✓Quarterly publishing and refresh plan

Process

A straightforward sequence

The exact scope is agreed before work begins, but the shape stays consistent.

  1. 01

    Inventory

    Understand what exists, what performs, and what creates overlap.

  2. 02

    Model

    Define clusters, page roles, and the internal links that connect them.

  3. 03

    Brief

    Convert research into clear instructions for writers and designers.

  4. 04

    Review

    Measure outcomes and refresh rather than endlessly adding new pages.

Common questions

Useful detail before you get in touch

Do you write the content?

Writing can be included, but many teams use the strategy and briefs with their own writers.

How many articles should we publish?

The right cadence depends on quality, capacity, and the opportunity. A maintainable plan beats an aggressive one.
