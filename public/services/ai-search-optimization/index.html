AI Search Optimisation (AEO & GEO)

AI answers are built from the same web search engines already index. The work is making your business easy to identify, easy to quote and present in the sources AI systems trust.

Focused scopePractical deliverablesClear handover

Best for

Businesses with sound SEO basics that want to appear in AI Overviews and assistant answers for their category.

In short

AI search optimisation helps Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity identify your business and cite it. Google says it is still SEO; the emphasis shifts to an unambiguous entity, direct answers on your pages, open crawl access for search bots and mentions in the sources AI answers draw on.

What is covered

Practical work, clearly scoped

AI search optimisation for Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity: entity clarity, answer-first content, crawl access and citations.

01

Entity clarity

One consistent name, description and set of profiles, with Organization or Person structured data that matches the visible page.

02

Answer-first content

Direct 40–60-word answers to the questions buyers ask, followed by detail, tables and visible FAQs.

03

Crawl access

Confirm search and citation bots (Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot) are not blocked by robots.txt or CDN bot settings.

04

Citation sources

Earn listings where AI answers draw from — credible directories, industry lists and review profiles — without self-ranking ‘top 10’ pages.

05

Measurement

Track AI Overview and AI Mode appearances in Search Console, AI referrals in analytics and a fixed monthly prompt set.

Common questions

Useful detail before you get in touch

Is AI search optimisation different from SEO?

Mostly not. Google’s own guidance (May 2026) says optimising for its generative AI features is still SEO and needs no special files or markup. What changes is emphasis: entity clarity and third-party corroboration matter more.

Does an llms.txt file help?

There is no measurable evidence yet. A study of roughly 300,000 domains found no relationship between having llms.txt and being cited by AI systems. It is harmless, but it is not where results come from.

Can you guarantee an AI Overview citation?

No. What I can show is a client cited in Google’s AI Overview for “MEP contractors in India” alongside national firms (25 November 2026), and the method behind it.
