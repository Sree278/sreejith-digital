Questions

Useful answers before you get in touch

What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant finds what is stopping a website from being found and trusted, sets priorities, and helps ship the fixes — technical, content and local — then measures the effect on enquiries. You deal with one accountable specialist rather than an account manager.

Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?

Yes. I’m based in Chennai and work remotely with businesses across India. I have pages for Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kerala and Tamil Nadu — plus eight Kerala cities and Coimbatore, Madurai and Salem — each with an honest status.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody controls Google’s rankings. What I show instead is dated client results — including the terms that haven’t reached page 1 yet — and the method behind them.

How long does SEO take for a business like mine?

Brand searches and niche long-tail terms can move within weeks to a few months; competitive city terms usually take six months or more. The case study shows one client reaching page 1 for five terms in about three months, but every market is different.

What is AI search optimisation, and do I need it?

It is making sure Google’s AI Overviews and assistants like ChatGPT can identify your business and cite it. Google says it is still SEO; the extra emphasis is entity clarity and third-party mentions. If your buyers ask AI tools for shortlists, it matters.
