Case study · MEP contractor

From brand-only searches to page 1: multi-city SEO for an MEP contractor

Mindtech Solutions is an MEP, HVAC and fit-out contractor working across India. When the baseline was recorded in August 2026, Google showed the site almost only to people who already knew the name. By November it held page-1 positions in two markets and appeared in Google’s AI Overview for MEP contractors in India.

The starting point

The site existed and Google could reach it, but almost nobody found it without already knowing the company’s name.

  • Google Search Console showed the site mainly for brand searches — people typing the company name.
  • None of the client’s 15 priority commercial keywords had produced a single reportable impression.
  • Search Console still listed several commercial pages as discovered but not yet indexed.
  • Enquiries arrived by phone and email with no record of which page or search produced them.

The goal

Rank for every core service in each target city

Seven services — MEP, HVAC, fire protection, electrical, HVAC AMC, building automation and retail fit-out — across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Delhi NCR, plus visibility as a national contractor.

What we did

The work, in the order it happened

Each step shipped through a reviewed release with automated checks.

  1. Aug 2026

    Indexation and keyword baseline

    Audited crawl and indexation, confirmed every commercial page had one H1, a self-referencing canonical and sitemap inclusion, and recorded a keyword baseline before changing anything.

  2. Aug 2026

    Money-page metadata

    Aligned titles, descriptions and H1s on the four priority service pages, then verified the live pages byte-for-byte against the approved source.

  3. Sep 2026

    Privacy-safe lead tracking

    Consent-based GA4 and Microsoft Clarity, a reference number on every enquiry and source and landing-page fields — proved end to end with controlled test enquiries.

  4. Sep 2026

    Commercial SEO on the money pages

    Rewrote the commercial pages around buyer intent and added the sections buyers look for before they ask for a quote.

  5. Oct 2026

    Proof beside the claim

    Approved project cards, an ISO 9001:2015 certification link and FAQs with matching structured data on the Bengaluru MEP page, plus contextual links from related pages. A project write-up gained Article and VideoObject structured data.

  6. Oct–Nov 2026

    Design system and safe releases

    A design-token system with automated consistency checks, then release automation with a tested rollback, so SEO changes could ship often without breaking the site.

  7. Nov 2026

    City pages that pass the test

    Hyderabad, Chennai and Delhi NCR pages built on local authorities, local sectors and an honest line about where work was done. Thin service × city combinations were held back or redirected instead of published.

  8. Nov 2026

    Home page goes national

    Retargeted the home page to India with a headline that names no city, so it stopped competing with the Bengaluru page for the same searches.

  9. Nov 2026

    AI search corroboration

    When a business directory appeared among the sources of the AI Overview, the client was submitted to it to strengthen independent corroboration.

How it worked

The method behind the results

01

One page per intent

Each service and city page has a single job. The home page claims India; each city page claims its city.

02

The city-name test

A city page ships only if swapping the city name would make it false. Otherwise it is held back or merged.

03

Proof next to claims

Approved projects, certifications and video sit on the page that makes the claim, mirrored in structured data.

04

Releases with evidence

Every change goes through a reviewed pull request, automated checks, a staging preview and a rollback point.

A logged-out Google search for “MEP contractors in India” showed an AI Overview listing five “Top Pan-India MEP Contractors”: Sterling and Wilson, Blue Star, UMPESL (Voltas), L&T Construction — and Mindtech Solutions, with a citation link to its website. What I’d do differently

Start third-party corroboration earlier. The AI Overview’s sources included a business directory and an industry list; listings like that take weeks to approve, so they should begin alongside the on-page work, not after it.

Collect Google Business Profile facts in week one. The client is not yet in any map pack, and profile work depends on business inputs that take time to gather.
