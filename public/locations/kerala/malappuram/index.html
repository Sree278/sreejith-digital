Local market

What Malappuram businesses need from search

The search work that pays off depends on how Malappuram businesses sell — and to whom.

Sector

Ayurveda

Kottakkal, home to the Arya Vaidya Sala since 1902, anchors a cluster of Ayurveda hospitals, clinics and product makers. Treatment pages need to answer international enquiries; product pages need to rank nationally.

Sector

Education

The University of Calicut sits in Malappuram district, and colleges, schools and job-oriented course providers compete for the same students. Course pages with fees, intakes and outcomes win over brochure-style pages.

Sector

Hospitals

Perinthalmanna is known as a hospital town, and patients travel from neighbouring districts. Department and doctor pages, plus profiles that match, help those patients choose before they travel.

Sector

Trade, property and construction

Gulf earnings fund much of the district’s construction and property market, and the international airport at Karipur is in the district. Buyers abroad need clear project details and remote-friendly enquiry paths.

How people in Malappuram search

Many decisions are made by family members in the Gulf, on Gulf time. Pages should answer what a remote decision-maker asks — fees, documents, timelines — and enquiry forms should work well on a phone.

Searches spread across the district’s towns rather than centring on Malappuram town: Manjeri, Tirur, Perinthalmanna, Kottakkal and Kondotty all have their own demand. Kottakkal already shows up in Google’s suggestions for SEO searches.

Questions

SEO in Malappuram: common questions

My customers’ families decide from the Gulf. What should the website do?

Answer their questions without a phone call: prices or fee ranges, documents, timelines and how payment works from abroad. Then track enquiries by country, so you can see how much of your business the Gulf audience drives.

Is Malayalam content worth it in Malappuram?

Check your Search Console queries first. If people search in Malayalam or Manglish for your services, a few well-written Malayalam pages can work; translating the whole site rarely pays off.

Do you work with businesses in Tirur, Manjeri and Perinthalmanna?

Yes. A business that genuinely serves several towns should cover each one properly on its own site. A town gets its own page only when people search for it and the page has something specific to say.

Do you have an office in Malappuram?

No. I’m from Thrissur and based in Chennai. I work with Malappuram businesses remotely, in Malayalam or English, and can meet in person when I travel to Kerala. I won’t list an address I don’t have.
