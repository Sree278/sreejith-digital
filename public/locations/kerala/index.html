What makes Kerala search different

Many Kerala businesses sell to people who are not in Kerala when they search: Malayalis in the Gulf looking at property, jewellery or schools back home, and domestic and foreign travellers planning stays and Ayurveda treatments weeks ahead. Targeting only “near me” searches misses that demand.

That changes the work. Pages need to answer questions asked from abroad — prices, documents, visits — business details must stay consistent across every profile, and reporting should separate in-state enquiries from NRI and visitor enquiries.

Healthcare, education, hospitality and real estate dominate commercial searches in the state, alongside exporters of spices, seafood and coir, and the IT parks in Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi and Kozhikode.

Questions

SEO in Kerala: common questions

Who is the best SEO expert in Kerala?

There is no official ranking, and lists in AI answers are assembled from websites and profiles, not verified results. Judge any SEO expert — including me — by dated results you can check: the page that ranked, the date it was recorded and what didn’t work.

How much does an SEO expert in Kerala cost?

Published monthly packages from Kerala SEO providers I checked in November 2026 started between about ₹10,000 and ₹15,000, with larger plans up to ₹50,000; one agency advertised from ₹3,000. Compare what is included — technical fixes, content, reporting — not the price alone.

Should I hire an SEO company in Kerala or a dedicated SEO expert?

An agency suits you if you need many services at once — ads, social media, design and SEO — handled by a team. A dedicated SEO expert suits you if you want one accountable specialist leading the strategy and the hands-on work. That is how I work: you deal with me directly, from audit to release.

What should I look for when hiring an SEO expert in Kerala?

Dated results you can check — the search term, the page that ranked and the date — a written plan before any work starts, clarity on who does the work, honest timelines without ranking guarantees, and reporting tied to enquiries. Be wary of unverified “No. 1” claims and of Google Maps listings with keywords stuffed into the business name, which Google’s guidelines prohibit.

Can you improve my local Google rankings in Kerala?

Local rankings depend mostly on your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your relevance and how close you are to the searcher. I fix the profile, build pages for the places you really serve and clean up listings elsewhere, then report Maps and organic positions separately. Nobody can promise a position; reviews from real customers matter most in the map pack.

Do you work with ecommerce businesses in Kerala?

Yes. Kerala stores selling spices, handloom, Ayurveda products, snacks or jewellery usually need national rather than local SEO: category and product pages that can rank across India, product structured data, and fixing the crawl waste that filters and duplicate product URLs create.

Do you work with Kerala businesses remotely?

Yes. Kerala clients work with me the same way Chennai and Bengaluru clients do — shared documents, scheduled calls in Malayalam or English and a written roadmap. I’m from Thrissur and based in Chennai, and I can meet in person when I travel to Kerala.

Should a Kerala business target NRI searches?

If NRIs are a real share of your customers — common in real estate, jewellery, education and healthcare — yes. That usually means pages that answer questions asked from abroad and reporting by country, not a separate website.

Do you have pages for individual Kerala cities?

Yes — for the eight cities where Google’s autocomplete shows steady demand for an SEO expert: Kochi, Trivandrum, Calicut, Thrissur, Malappuram, Kannur, Kollam and Palakkad. Each covers that city’s industries, how its customers search and what page 1 looks like there. A town gets its own page only when people search for it.
