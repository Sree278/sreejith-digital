Local market

What Kannur businesses need from search

The search work that pays off depends on how Kannur businesses sell — and to whom.

Sector

Handloom and home furnishings

Kannur’s woven home furnishings are exported worldwide, but many weavers and cooperatives depend on buyers who already know them. Product pages with materials, sizes and care details can reach retail and trade buyers directly.

Sector

Theyyam season and beaches

Visitors plan trips around the Theyyam season and beaches like Muzhappilangad. Homestays that publish useful, dated guides before the season can win bookings directly instead of paying platform commissions.

Sector

Hospitals and education

The medical college at Pariyaram, Kannur University and private hospitals serve north Kerala. Accurate department, course and doctor pages matter more than a long home page.

Sector

Gulf-linked trade and property

Like much of north Kerala, Kannur has a large Gulf diaspora, which shapes demand for property, gold and construction. Pages need to answer buyers who are not in Kannur when they search.

How people in Kannur search

Kannur district searches spread across several towns — Thalassery, Payyanur, Taliparamba and Mattannur near the airport — and many people search the town, not the district.

Tourism searches are strongly seasonal and timed around festivals. Content published after the season starts is usually too late to rank for it.

Questions

SEO in Kannur: common questions

Can a Kannur handloom unit sell online through search?

Yes, if the product pages do the selling: real photos, materials, sizes, care instructions, wholesale terms and shipping details. Marketplaces help with reach; your own site builds the brand and the margin.

When should homestays publish Theyyam content?

Well before the season — a guide that is live by early autumn has time to rank. Keep it factual and dated, and link it to the rooms and booking page.

Do you cover Thalassery, Payyanur and Taliparamba?

Yes. I work with businesses anywhere in Kannur district, remotely. If you serve several towns, each needs proper coverage on your site, not a copied page.

Do you have an office in Kannur?

No. I’m from Thrissur and based in Chennai. I work with Kannur businesses remotely, in Malayalam or English, and can meet in person when I travel to Kerala. I won’t list an address I don’t have.
