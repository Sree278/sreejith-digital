Local market

What Kochi businesses need from search

The search work that pays off depends on how Kochi businesses sell — and to whom.

Sector

IT and startups

Infopark in Kakkanad and the startup campus at Kalamassery are full of firms whose buyers are in Bengaluru, the Gulf or abroad. They rarely need “Kochi” in their keywords; they need pages built around what those buyers search.

Sector

Port, logistics and exports

Cochin Port, the Vallarpadam container terminal and the Spices Board and MPEDA head offices make Kochi an export city. Overseas buyers search by product, grade and certification, so specification pages matter more than a home page.

Sector

Retail and real estate

From Edappally to Kakkanad, apartment projects and large stores compete for the same local searches. Project pages with registration details, and answers for NRI buyers, convert better than generic listings.

Sector

Tourism and healthcare

Fort Kochi and Mattancherry stays compete with booking platforms, and large hospitals draw patients from across Kerala and the Gulf. Both win with their own detailed pages and a well-kept Google profile.

How people in Kochi search

Customers use three names for the same place: Kochi, Cochin and Ernakulam. A page should lead with the one your customers use — your Search Console queries will tell you — and mention the others naturally, not repeat all three in every heading.

For local services, the locality often carries more intent than the city: “Kakkanad”, “Edappally” or “Vyttila” searches come from people ready to visit. Those belong in service pages and in your Google Business Profile service areas.

Questions

SEO in Kochi: common questions

Should my Kochi business target “Kochi”, “Cochin” or “Ernakulam”?

All three are searched. Lead with the name your customers use (check your Search Console queries), mention the others once where it reads naturally, and use locality names like Kakkanad or Edappally where you actually serve. Don’t build a separate page for each name; that is duplication.

Should I hire an SEO company in Kochi or a dedicated expert?

If you want ads, social media, design and SEO run by one team, an agency fits. If you want one accountable person doing the strategy and the hands-on SEO, a dedicated expert fits. I lead every engagement myself and work with Kochi businesses remotely.

Can an Infopark company rank outside Kerala?

Yes, and that is usually the right goal. Product and service firms in Kochi rarely need the city in their keywords. They need pages built around what buyers in Bengaluru, the Gulf or overseas type, plus proof those buyers trust.

How long does SEO take for a Kochi business?

With a sound site, local service terms often move within two to three months and settle over four to six. In my published case study, five commercial terms reached page 1 in about three months — in other markets, so treat it as a reference, not a promise.

Do you have an office in Kochi?

No. I’m from Thrissur and based in Chennai. I work with Kochi businesses remotely, in Malayalam or English, and can meet in person when I travel to Kerala. I won’t list an address I don’t have.
