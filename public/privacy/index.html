Privacy Policy

What this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated 2026-11-27

This site collects only what you choose to send, and it loads analytics only if you allow them.

Who is responsible

Sreejith CU, trading as Sreejith Digital, Chennai, India, is responsible for personal data collected through this website. Contact: hello@sreejithdigital.com.

Information you send

The enquiry and free-review forms ask for your name, email, website, the service you need, a budget range and an optional message. They are used only to reply to you and, if you ask for one, to prepare your review. You give consent by ticking the box on the form, as required by India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The contact page can show my phone number after you enter an email address. That address is checked for validity (including a DNS lookup of its domain), your number is checked for format, and a notice of the request, with your email address and number, is sent to my inbox so I can follow up. It is not used for marketing and is deleted with other enquiries.

Form submissions are processed by Cloudflare, which hosts this site, and delivered to my inbox. If the form cannot reach the server, your own email app prepares the message instead, and your email provider processes it under its own terms.

How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up — normally no longer than 12 months — unless we start working together, in which case they are kept for the length of the engagement and any period the law requires.

Analytics and cookies

Analytics are off by default. Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity load only after you choose “Allow analytics”, and Google Analytics and Clarity then set cookies to measure visits and how pages are used. Advertising cookies stay off. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. The site’s own scripts store only that choice in your browser.

Technical information

The hosting provider may process routine technical data such as IP addresses, request times and requested URLs to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse. This data is not used for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, withdraw consent, or raise a concern, by emailing hello@sreejithdigital.com. I will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If the way this site handles data changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
