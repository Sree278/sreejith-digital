The locations hub lists these as planned, **without publishing thin placeholder pages**:

Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Coimbatore, Gurugram, Noida, Thiruvananthapuram, Indore.

A new page should be published only after documenting:

1. The real audience and commercial intent in that market.
2. The service or industry cluster the page supports.
3. Genuinely local examples, constraints or search behaviour.
4. Internal links to relevant service and industry pages.
5. A distinct conversion action or enquiry context.

Do not create 20 near-identical city pages. This avoids doorway-page patterns and scaled low-value content.

## 9. Guardrails

- Do not buy bulk backlinks.
- Do not publish mass-produced city pages.
- Do not claim offices in cities where there is no genuine presence.
- Do not guarantee rankings.
- Do not invent client names, case studies, testimonials or performance numbers.
- Do not add analytics or third-party scripts without updating the privacy policy.
- Do not let `www` and apex both serve duplicate content; keep one canonical host.
- Do not confuse `sreejith-digital.com` with the registered `sreejithdigital.com`.
